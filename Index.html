📖 Grammar Review: Present Continuous

1. What is Present Continuous?

We use it for actions that are happening right now (at the moment of speaking).

Look! The cat is sleeping.I am reading now.

Time words: now, right now, at the moment, today, Look!, Listen!

2. How to use am, is, and are

I → am  |  He, She, It and one person/thing → is  |  You, We, They and many people/things → are

I am dancing.The dog is running.The children are playing.

3. Positive sentences

Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing

She is dancing.They are playing.

4. Negative sentences

Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ing

The rabbit is not sleeping.They are not playing.

Short forms: is not = isn't, are not = aren't.

5. Questions

Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing?

Is the cat running?Are the children dancing?

Short answers: Yes, it is. / No, they aren't.

6. Spelling rules for -ing

Most verbs: add -ing → playing, reading

Verb ends in e: drop the e → writing, dancing, making

Short verb with 1 vowel + 1 consonant: double the last letter → running, swimming, sitting

7. Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

Present Simple = habits and routines (every day, usually, always): I walk to school every day.

Present Continuous = happening now (now, Look!, Listen!): I am walking in the park now.
